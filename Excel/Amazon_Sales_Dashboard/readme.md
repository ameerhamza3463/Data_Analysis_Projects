# 🛒 Amazon Sales Dashboard

This project features an Excel-based dashboard designed to analyze Amazon sales data across various products, regions, and months. The dashboard transforms raw sales figures into actionable insights, helping stakeholders make informed decisions to boost performance.

## 📁 Project Overview

The dashboard provides a visual and interactive summary of key sales metrics, such as:
- Total Revenue and Units Sold
- Category-wise and Region-wise Performance
- Top Performing Products
- Monthly Trends and Seasonality

## 💡 Objective

To provide a comprehensive, user-friendly sales analytics that enables deeper insight into sales trends, product performance, and market opportunities on the Amazon platform.

## 🔧 Techniques and Tools Used

| Category                | Techniques/Features Applied                                   |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| 📊 Data Analysis        | Pivot Tables                                                  |
| 📈 Visualization        | Pie Charts, Line Charts, KPI Tiles                            |
| 🖌️ Custom Design        | Intuitive layout, consistent color palette, and clean visuals |

## 📈 Key Features

- Overview of total sales and profits at a glance
- Comparison of product categories and regions

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the `Amazon Sales Data.xlsx` file in Excel (2016 or later).
2. Use Sidebar to interact with the dashboard.
3. Read charts and KPIs to interpret business trends and spot opportunities.

## 🏁 Outcome

This dashboard delivers a full view of Amazon's sales dynamics, offering both high-level summaries and granular detail for deeper business insights.
